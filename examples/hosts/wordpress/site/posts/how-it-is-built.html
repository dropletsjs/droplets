Every part of the shop is a droplet: a small server-rendered piece of UI with its own framework. The filter is Hono JSX, the product list Svelte, the cart React, the product page Handlebars, the comments Solid and the admin Vue.

WordPress fetches each page's fragments on the server, all at once, and the theme lays them out like any other content. The browser then talks to the shop through this site, so its cookies are this site's own.
