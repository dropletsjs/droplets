This is a WordPress site with a stock theme, Twenty Twenty-Five. Its pages, menu and journal are ordinary WordPress content, and so is the shop's catalogue: the products are posts with their price and stock, their pictures are in the Media Library, their reviews are WordPress comments.

The shop's interface is a droplets server running beside the site, which reads the catalogue through WordPress's REST API and keeps only the carts itself. The Gallery and Cart pages hold the shop's shortcodes, and the Droplets Embed plugin puts the shop's server-rendered fragments where the shortcodes are. Stock and prices are managed in WordPress's own dashboard, under Shop, by whoever is logged in to WordPress as an administrator: there is no other login.
